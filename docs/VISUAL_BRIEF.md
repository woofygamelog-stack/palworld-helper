# Palworld Helper visual brief

- Mood: bright open-world exploration, friendly discovery, and practical field equipment.
- Palette: ocean cyan and sky blue for navigation, grass green for discovery, warm gold for rewards and focus, coral for alerts, and deep navy for structure.
- Materials: enamel badges, translucent map glass, pale parchment panels, soft cloud light, and restrained topographic lines.
- Shape language: generous rounded panels with a dark framed edge, compass arcs, map pins, floating-island silhouettes, and compact HUD-like controls.
- Density: information-first cards and tools remain calm; decorative atmosphere stays in page backgrounds, headers, hero art, and empty space.
- Motion: small hover lift and highlight only, with reduced-motion support preserved.
- Independence: evoke Palworld's adventurous world tone without copying its logo, characters, Pal Spheres, interface, or illustrations.
